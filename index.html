PLEASE READ BEFORE RENTING

Before You Rent

A few things to know before reserving your C-LHOUETTE look.

Valid ID Required

A valid government-issued ID is required privately after reservation approval and before dress release.

₱1,000 Security Deposit

Required before dress release. Refunded immediately after return inspection, less any applicable charges.

Late Returns

An additional ₱200 is charged for each day past the agreed return date.

Dress Care & Damages

Return dresses in good condition. Alterations are not allowed. Damage charges may apply. Please check dress measurements carefully.

Payment & Shipping

After submitting an inquiry, contact our official page to coordinate payment and delivery. Customers shoulder applicable delivery and return shipping fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

Is my reservation confirmed immediately?

No. Reservations are confirmed only after approval and payment verification.

When will my security deposit be refunded?

Immediately after the returned dress passes inspection, less any applicable deductions.

What happens if I damage the dress?

Charges may apply for damage beyond normal wear. Specific damage fees will be provided before rental confirmation.

How is my valid ID protected?

IDs are collected privately and stored with restricted staff access. They are deleted within 7 days after rental completion and resolution of outstanding issues, unless longer retention is required by law.
